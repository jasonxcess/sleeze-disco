ARROWS / D-PAD: Move across the dancefloor
[J] / □ PUNCH: Fast beat jab  |  [K] / △ KICK: Heavy knockback
[L] / ○ THROW: Ranged neon drumstick
[SPACE] / ✕ DODGE: Dash through red attacks
[S] / L1 PARRY: Deflect & stun attackers
[F] / ♪ DANCE: Pure dance move — no damage, builds Fever + style variety
[E] / R1 TAKEDOWN: Spend 1 ★ token (earned every 12 combo — buy more slots in the Green Room) for a cinematic finisher
BOSS MOVES: beat a chapter boss to take its move — Ch.1 unlocks DASH STRIKE (the dash itself hits), Ch.2 unlocks SPINBACK KICK (wider, harder kicks)
FEVER: on-beat hits + dance fill it — at 100% FEVER RUSH starts on its own: faster attacks, denser drums, wild lights
GOLD COUNTER: parry while the gold COUNTER prompt is up to stun & smash
OFF-BEAT: swings snap to the next beat and still land — only getting hit breaks your combo
MUSIC: story chapters run on Sleeze's own tracks — My Luva (Ch.1), DCeased (Ch.2), Shit Talkin (Ch.3). Endless lets you pick any of the five, or import your own MP3/WAV — the beat gets read off the file and it loops under the fight (web only; the Vita uses the preset track BPMs)
