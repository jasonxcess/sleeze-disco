## Motion: Adobe Mixamo

Mixamo animations are free to use in games under Adobe's Mixamo terms. They can't be redistributed as standalone animation files; here they only ship baked into the character .glb files.

- Already in the project (`files/mixamo/`): Kicking, Center Block (parry), Uppercut (counter + every 3rd punch), Getting Up, Spin Flip Kick and Meia Lua De Compasso (finishers), Hip Hop Dancing (crowd).
- New for this build, downloaded Nov 12, 2026 on the user's Mixamo account (Y Bot, FBX, without skin, 30 fps; saved in `web/downloads/mixamo/`): Fighting Idle, Walking (in place), Running (in place), Fast Run (in place, the sprint), Punching / jab, Punching / cross, Hit Reaction, Head Hit (also mirrored to give a left and a right flinch), Receiving An Uppercut (rear flinch), Knocked Out, Taunt (boxing).
- Retargeting was done offline in Blender with `tools/char_build.py`. It's a direction + frame retarget that works across the T-pose, A-pose and Vex rest poses.
- Unused from `files/mixamo/`: Armada, Flair, Flair (1), Head Spinning, Head Spinning (1), Leg Sweep.
